# Findings

_Generated by `src/analysis.py`. 36,308 multi-product orders (UCI Online Retail II, Dec 2009 - Dec 2011). Minimum support: product in 300+ orders, pair in 150+ orders._

## 1. Minimum support is not optional
- With no threshold, products sold only once produce **162 rules** with 100% confidence and a lift of 36,308. They are artefacts of a single order.
- With the thresholds: 9,274 product pairs -> 18,548 directional rules; **2,055** meet attach rate >= 30% and lift >= 3.
- Pair counts from the SQL Apriori query and an independent Python sparse-matrix count match exactly.

## 2. Bundles that hold up
- Strongest: **Regency Tea Plate Pink -> Regency Tea Plate Green**: 91% of orders with the first also contain the second (lift 87x, 278 orders).
- 88% of strong rules keep a lift of 2x+ in **both** 2010 and 2011 baskets when recomputed year by year, so they are not one-season effects.
- 71% of strong rules pair products in the same category - mostly colour/design variants of one item (e.g. the same bag in different prints). These are 'complete the set' prompts rather than cross-category bundles.
- Even the top rules that cross keyword categories are mostly sibling designs (Kitchen Metal Sign -> Bathroom Metal Sign; Gardeners Kneeling Pad Cup Of Tea -> Gardeners Kneeling Pad Keep Calm). Real cross-sell candidates sit further down `reports/tables/cross_category_rules.csv`; grouping products into families before mining would be the next step.
- Largest attach gap among strong rules: 2,631 orders contained Jumbo Bag Red Retrospot without Jumbo Storage Bag Suki (attach rate 33%). The Excel Bundle Explorer turns any such gap into a what-if revenue figure from an assumed conversion share.

## 3. Who the baskets belong to
- Orders with 11+ different products are 63% of orders but 78% of merchandise revenue - consistent with the source describing many customers as wholesalers. Bundle prompts matter most in this assortment-building context.
- Category-level attach is high everywhere (large mixed baskets), so product-level rules are the useful level.

## 4. Customer value
- **Champions** (recent and frequent) are 25% of identified customers and 69% of their revenue.
- **Loyal - slipping** (frequent buyers, not seen recently): 360 customers, 6% of revenue - the win-back list.
- 12-month merchandise spend (before cancellations) of newly acquired customers ranges from GBP 1,020 to GBP 1,958 (average per customer by acquisition quarter); 71% place a second order within a year. The Dec-2009 group is the pre-existing base and is shown separately.

## Caveats
- Association is not causation: a high lift says two products are bought together, not that recommending one will sell the other. Test prompts with an A/B split before rolling out.
- Product categories come from keyword rules on descriptions (`src/etl.py`); 'Other' is ~9% of revenue.
